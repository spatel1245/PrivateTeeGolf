First visit? Start here

Your visit in 4 quick steps

Everything starts automatically. Here’s all you need to know.

  1. 1Find the entrance
  2. 2Enter your code
  3. 3Place your ball
  4. 4Wait for green

Getting inside

Find the door. Enter your code.

Your 6-digit code arrives by text about 5 minutes before your booking. It works at your exact start time.

✓

Once you’re inside

Don’t turn anything on.

The computer, projector, TV, and simulator start automatically.

Before every swing

Place your ball in the hitting area

The orange outline shows where the launch monitor can see your ball, so please leave the mat where it is.

Ready check

Wait for the green light

The overhead Uneekor logo must glow green before every swing.

Using GSPro

Move the mouse left

Slide the mouse all the way left until it appears on the projector. Choose Local Match to play a course, or choose Practice to use the driving range.

When time is up

Clean up and head out

  • ✓ Finish by the end of your booking.
  • ✓ Take your belongings and clean up.
  • ✓ The simulator shuts down automatically.
